I am a 3rd year student at the University of Cambridge studying Computer Science. I also work part time for Jetbrains as a campus ambassador for the Universities of Oxford and Cambridge.

I'm interested in planetary computing. After my first year I did a summer research internship at the University of Cambridge Planetary Computing Department where I built an open source tool for 3D printing terrain.

I love writing software for robotics and have worked on some cool projects such as a self playing chess board and a haunted typewriter.

I also enjoy analyzing prediction markets. I'm fascinated by how they quantify public uncertainty, as well as the structural inefficiencies that can be leveraged.
